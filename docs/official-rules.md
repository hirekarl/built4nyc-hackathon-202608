# The New York Public Library's Built for NYC: AI Hackathon — Official Rules

## Hackathon Prompt

Using generative AI and "vibe coding," your mission is to develop a web app, assisted by AI, that meets a challenge that NYC is facing — for example, mapping food deserts, organizing neighborhood park utilization, or improving local access to city resources.

### Submission Criteria

All submissions must incorporate "vibe coding" to develop a web app that meets a challenge that NYC is facing, to be in the running for placement in the main category; winners will be chosen and ranked based on how novel and impactful the submitted app serves this purpose.

- To meet the criteria for **"Best Beginner Project"**, entrants must have no prior professional vibe coding experience and must not have previously participated in any other vibe coding hackathon or contest.
- To meet the criteria for **"Best Use of NYC Open Data"**, you must use a resource made available by [NYC Open Data](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/) in a novel way that serves to solve a common problem or make certain information more accessible to New Yorkers.

Submissions will be selected for publication on the event Devpost in the sole discretion and judgment of the committee based on:

1. Adherence to the NYPL's Built for NYC: AI Hackathon prompt

3. Potential impact
