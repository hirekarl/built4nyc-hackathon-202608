## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Are there teams? And if so, how are teams created?

Teams are not required or assigned for the event, although if a team naturally forms and wants to collaborate, it is allowed. However, please note that only one (1) person will be able to submit for the group, and that person will be responsible for the equal distribution of any prize money won as a result of that effort. We would, however, advise that teams do not exceed 4 members in total for the sake of managing this process.

### Does all work have to be completed on location?

No. While we are providing the space for all attendees to work on the 7th floor at the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturday (8/15) and 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM on Sunday (8/16) when final submissions are due, you are welcome to work from elsewhere in between the opening ceremony and the final submission to the Devpost — as long as you check in on the first day and are back at the 7th floor event space to present your submitted project through the judging phase, which will begin at 2:15 PM on Sunday, August 16th.
