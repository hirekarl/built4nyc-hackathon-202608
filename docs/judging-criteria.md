# Judging Criteria (Presentation Slide)

Source: slide shown during the event presentation ("Judging Criteria — to consider as you prepare your presentation"), captured 2026-08-15. This expands on the three criteria listed in [`official-rules.md`](./official-rules.md#L50-L55) into the four-point rubric judges will actually use — worth designing the demo and presentation around.

## Criteria

- **AI Usage & Technology** — Did the team utilize AI tools responsibly? Did they keep a human in the loop?
- **Design** — Did the team put thought into the user experience? How well designed is the interface?
- **Completion & Theme** — Does the hack work? Did the team achieve everything they wanted? Did they adhere to the theme?
- **Learning** — Did the team stretch themselves? Did they try to learn something new?

## Implications for our submission

- Keep the human-in-the-loop story visible (e.g. review/approval steps, not fully autonomous AI actions) — judges are explicitly scoring this.
- Polish the UI/UX, not just functionality — design is a standalone criterion.
- Make sure the demo runs end-to-end without visible bugs, and stays on-theme (an NYC community challenge).
- Have a short, honest note ready on what was new/stretchy for the team (new framework, new API, new domain, etc.) for the "Learning" criterion.
