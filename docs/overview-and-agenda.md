# Built for NYC: AI Hackathon

**August 15th & 16th, 2026** Presented by TechConnect, The New York Public Library, and partner organization Major League Hacking (MLH)

Are you "Built for NYC"? What if you could use code to help your local community? The New York Public Library and partner organization Major League Hacking (MLH) are proud to present this free weekend-long hackathon at the Stavros Niarchos Foundation Library (SNFL).

## General Challenge

_All participants, by default, will be competing in this track._

Build a project **inspired by a challenge you notice in your community**. Prizes will be awarded for 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.

## Opt-In Challenges

### Best Beginner Project

A track for individuals or teams made up of at least 50% folks who attended the half-day "Learning Track" on Day 1.

### Best Use of NYC Open Data

This award is going to be given to a project that best utilizes an NYC Open Data set.
